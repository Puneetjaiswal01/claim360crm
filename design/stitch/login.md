Design a Claim 360 CRM sign-in page using DESIGN.md.

Desktop uses two balanced columns. On the left, use a deep navy story panel with
the existing Claim 360 brand, the headline "Every claim. A clearer way forward."
and a three-step illustrative recovery workflow: Organize, Connect, Recover.
Keep the illustration typographic and geometric, without invented metrics.

The right side is a quiet light form panel: welcome message, labeled work email
and password inputs, a password visibility toggle, validation message and a teal
"Sign in to workspace" button. Include a small local development indicator when
appropriate. Never show actual credentials. Support loading and error states.

On mobile, compress the story panel, keep the form central, and preserve useful
touch areas, readable labels, password-manager autocomplete and visible focus.
